Positioning

I don't just create content.
I think about why it should exist.

Good social content isn't an accident. It comes from understanding who you're speaking to, what they care about, and what format earns their attention — then producing it consistently and learning from what performs.

I approach social media end to end: from research and audience understanding through strategy, planning, hooks and scripting, into short-form production and publishing — then back to performance review and optimization. My work so far has been independent social media and content projects, which is where the process below has been built and tested.

The System

Social Media Management

“From content planning to publishing, I approach social media as a system — not a collection of random posts.”

  1. 01

    Research

    Audience, niche, trends, competitors and content opportunities.

  2. 02

    Strategy

    Content pillars, positioning, formats and posting direction.

  3. 03

    Plan

    Content calendars, topics, hooks, scripts and captions.

  4. 04

    Create

    Reels, visuals, promotional content and supporting creatives.

  5. 05

    Publish

    Consistent scheduling, publishing and platform-ready formatting.

  6. 06

    Analyze

    Review content performance and identify patterns.

  7. 07

    Optimize

    Improve future content based on what performs.

  8. ↺

    Then it repeats

    What performs feeds the next round of research. Consistency compounds.
